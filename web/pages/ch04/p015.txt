Draw

When an external force is applied to the pallets (a shock, for example) the pallet lever may move away from one or other of the banking pins (during the

supplementary arc) or it may cease to function normally (during the angle of lift)

There may therefore be contact between the tip of ; the guard pin and the edge of the safety roller, or between a horn and the impulse pin. This contact should be as brief as possible, otherwise the oscillator will no longer function properly (see 6.4. 1). :

|

The purpose of draw is therefore to push the lever against the banking pins during the supplementary arc and to bring it back quickly if the watch suffers a shock.

This is achieved by angling the locking plane of the pallet jewels, which gives us the angle of draw. When pressure is applied through a tooth of the

escape wheel the lever is thus returned to the banking. fig. 6-42

In effect, the force F: acting at right angles on the locking plane of the pallet jewel creates a moment of force acting at distance d, from the centre of rotation of the pallets. The effect of this moment of force returns the lever to the banking pin with a force Fe acting at distance d, (fig. 6-42).
