Definitions

During its working cycle the lever escapement produces five main sounds in the following order:

Unlocking: the impulse pin against one side of the fork notch (fig. 6-49).

Start of impulse by the wheel: locking beak of the tooth of the wheel against the impulse plane of the entry pallet jewel

(fig. 6-50).

Start of the balance impulse: the other side of the fork notch against the impulse

pin (fig. 6-51).

End of drop: the escape wheel tooth drops on to the locking plane of the exit pallet

jewel (fig. 6-52).

Run to the banking: the lever hits the banking pin (fig. 6-53).
