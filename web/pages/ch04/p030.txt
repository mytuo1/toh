This is a recoil escapement which is mainly found in very old pocket watches.

The escape wheel arbor is at 90° to the balance staff (verge). The latter carries two pallets which are separated by a distance corresponding to the escape wheel diameter. The balance staff, with integral pallets, takes the place of separate pallets (fig. 6-81).

As an escape wheel tooth contacts the first pallet, thus providing the necessary impulse for the balance to oscillate, the second pallet contacts the Opposite escape wheel tooth and virtually locks it.

The escape wheel recoils slightly as the balance oscillates, repelled by the second pallet. The recoil ceases as the balance commences its oscillation
