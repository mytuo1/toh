Stopping on the impulse plane

A watch is stopped on the impulse plane when it does not start automatically, after unlocking has occurred, while it is being wound (fig. 6-61). A tooth of the escape wheel presses on the impulse plane of one or other of the pallet jewels.

All watches stop on the impulse plane when the mainspring is hardly wound. After having wound the mainspring by 2 or 3 ratchet-wheel teeth a pin or a soft brush is used to move the balance until stopping on the impulse occurs.
