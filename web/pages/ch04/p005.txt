Sometimes the impulse pin is even fitted directly into the balance. In this case, part of the balance staff is machined to form the safety roller (fig. 6-16).

The roller table carries the impulse pin. A crescent or passing hollow in the rim of the safety roller allows the guard pin to pass.

The impulse pin is normally made out of synthetic ruby. Its cross section may vary, but in principle it is semicircular (fig. 6-17). It may, however, be triangular (fig. 6-18).
